Introduction

ProdPulse is an internal industrial production and workforce management application operated by Technico Industries Ltd. (“Technico Industries”, “we”, “us”, or “our”). It is designed to digitize production and operational records that were previously maintained on paper and in spreadsheets, and it is intended for use by authorized employees and supervisors of Technico Industries Ltd.

By using ProdPulse, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy. If you have any questions, please contact us using the details in the Contact Us section.

At a glance

  • No sale of data. We do not sell your information to third parties.
  • No advertising. ProdPulse does not contain advertisements.
  • Workplace use only. ProdPulse is for authorized personnel and is not a social or public communication app.
  • Deletion on request. You can request account or data deletion by email.

01Information We Collect

Depending on how ProdPulse is configured and used within your organization, the application may process the following categories of information. We aim to collect only the information required to operate the application and support its intended business purposes.

Account information

  • Name
  • Employee or supervisor ID / code
  • Email address
  • Login credentials and authentication identifiers
  • Department and role information

Production and operational data

  • Production line information
  • Model / product information
  • Hourly production quantities
  • Production targets
  • Rejection quantities
  • Machine downtime
  • Maintenance and stop reasons
  • Production timestamps
  • Department information
  • Reports and KPI-related information

Technical information

  • Authentication information
  • Basic device and application information, where required for authentication, security, reliability, or troubleshooting

Production and operational data is entered by authorized users in the course of their work. Please avoid entering personal information into free-text fields (such as stop or maintenance reasons) unless it is necessary for operational purposes.

02How We Use Information

We use the information described above to:

  • Authenticate authorized users
  • Provide access to application features appropriate to each user’s role
  • Record and manage production operations
  • Calculate production targets and performance
  • Generate reports and analytics, including KPI dashboards and exported operational reports
  • Monitor production performance
  • Improve operational efficiency
  • Maintain the security of the application
  • Troubleshoot technical problems
  • Maintain and improve the application
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations

03Firebase Services

ProdPulse uses services provided by Google Firebase to operate the application, including:

Firebase Authentication
Used to verify the identity of authorized users and manage secure sign-in to the application.
Firebase Firestore
Used as the application’s cloud database to store and retrieve account, production, and operational records.

These services may process and store information as necessary for authentication, database functionality, security, and the operation of the application. Google’s handling of information is described in the Firebase Privacy and Security information (opens in a new tab) and the Google Privacy Policy (opens in a new tab).

04Data Sharing

  • We do not sell data. Technico Industries Ltd. does not sell information collected through ProdPulse to third parties.
  • Service providers. Information may be processed by service providers that are necessary to operate the application, such as Google Firebase services.
  • Authorized personnel. Information may be accessible to authorized personnel of Technico Industries Ltd. based on their role and responsibilities.
  • Legal and safety reasons. Information may be disclosed when required by law, or when necessary to protect the rights, safety, or security of Technico Industries Ltd., our users, or others.

05Data Security

Technico Industries Ltd. takes appropriate technical and organizational measures designed to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. Access to ProdPulse requires authentication, and access to information within the application is limited according to user roles and responsibilities.

While we work to protect information, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be entirely secure. Users are responsible for keeping their login credentials confidential and should notify us promptly of any suspected unauthorized access.

06Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as necessary for legitimate business, operational, legal, security, and record-keeping purposes. When information is no longer required for these purposes, we take reasonable steps to delete or anonymize it.

Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information and applicable legal or business requirements.

07Account and Data Deletion

You may request deletion of your ProdPulse account and/or your personal information at any time by contacting Technico Industries Ltd.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to ceo.office@technicoindustries.com, preferably from the email address associated with your ProdPulse account.
  2. Use the subject line “ProdPulse Account Deletion Request”.
  3. Include your full name, employee/supervisor ID or code, and the email address used for ProdPulse, and state whether you are requesting deletion of your account, your personal information, or both.

We may need to verify your identity before processing your request. Once verified, we will delete or anonymize your account and personal information, subject to applicable legal and business retention requirements. Certain information, such as production and operational records that form part of company business records, may be retained where required for legal, security, or legitimate business record-keeping purposes.

08Children's Privacy

ProdPulse is intended for use by authorized employees and supervisors of Technico Industries Ltd. It is not directed toward children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe that a child has provided personal information through ProdPulse, please contact us so that we can take appropriate action.

09International Users

ProdPulse relies on cloud services provided by Google Firebase. As a result, information may be processed and stored on servers operated by our service providers, which may be located outside the country in which you use the application. We handle information in accordance with this Privacy Policy and applicable law.

10Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the application, our practices, or applicable requirements. The updated version will be published on this webpage with a revised “Last Updated” date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
